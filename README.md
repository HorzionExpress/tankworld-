**IRON HORIZON — 装甲地平线**

*Co-developed with Zhuhang Studio (逐航工作室). Built in just over two weeks, because apparently we hate sleep.*

Ever wondered what happens when you throw WWII tanks, propeller fighters, modern air-defense missile systems, and a stealth bomber carrying a tactical nuke into the same browser tab? Neither did we — but we built it anyway. Welcome to **Iron Horizon**, a fully client-side 3D battlefield where absolutely everything runs inside a single HTML file. No downloads. No servers. No excuses.

**What's in the box (all 12 of them):**


- Six WWII tanks — T-34-85, Tiger I, IS-2, 59 式，Sherman, Panther — because who doesn't love clanking around in 60-year-old armor?

- Three flyable aircraft: the P-51D Mustang, the F4U-1 Corsair (yes, with the bent gull wings), and a B-25J Mitchell bomber.

- Two modern SAM systems: the Russian Tor-M2E (tracked, spinning radar, very dramatic) and China's HQ-17A (wheeled, phased-array, no spinning — it's too cool to spin).

- One B-2 Spirit stealth bomber armed with a B61-12 nuke. It's invisible to radar and extremely rude about it. 200 meters of "your whole team just ceased to exist."



**The juicy bits:** radar locking, missile interception, chaff countermeasures (the Tor and HQ-17A DO have G-key chaff — in real life they'd also like some close-defense guns, but hey, we're not the Pentagon), base 

capture, repair mechanics, achievements, a token unlock system, a full tutorial, and a working cheats menu because we respect your time.

**Tech stuff:** Three.js, procedural geometry (we sculpted every tank by hand out of boxes — fine art, honestly), fully synthesized WebAudio sound effects (zero audio files, 100% oscillators), dual-language UI (中文 / EN), 

and local save data.
